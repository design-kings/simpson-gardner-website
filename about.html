Our story.

Who we are

Family owned. Word-of-mouth built.

Joey Gardner has been building custom homes across North Texas since 2004. Simpson & Gardner was founded on a simple belief: bid the job right, guarantee the price, show up every day, and do right by every client. With over 200 homes completed across Decatur, Wise County, Bridgeport, Fort Worth, and Denton, that approach has kept the business growing — almost entirely through referrals.
