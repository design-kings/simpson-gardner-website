Crisp white stucco, black steel accents, and a stone base set the tone outside. Inside, white oak cabinetry, marble-look quartz, and brass fixtures carry it through every room. Bonita Creek

A floor-to-ceiling stacked stone fireplace anchors the vaulted great room beneath a tongue-and-groove wood ceiling and exposed beams. A knotty alder bar with granite counters and glass-front cabinets, wide-plank hardwood floors, and a primary suite with its own stone fireplace round out a home built for gathering. Outside, a deep covered porch on cedar posts looks over open pasture.
