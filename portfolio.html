Ball Parkway

Crisp white stucco, black steel accents, and a stone base set the tone outside. Inside, white oak cabinetry, marble-look quartz, and brass fixtures carry it through every room. Highlights include a floating white-oak staircase with a black steel rail, a sage green butler's pantry with a rolling library ladder and patterned tile, a freestanding soaking tub, a full outdoor kitchen, and a bunk room built for a crowd.

Bonita Creek

A floor-to-ceiling stacked stone fireplace anchors the vaulted great room beneath a tongue-and-groove wood ceiling and exposed beams. A knotty alder bar with granite counters and glass-front cabinets, wide-plank hardwood floors, and a primary suite with its own stone fireplace round out a home built for gathering. Outside, a deep covered porch on cedar posts looks over open pasture.

Briar Oak Estates

Hand-laid stone and brick, steep rooflines, and a copper-topped cupola give this home its old-world character from the end of a long brick drive. Inside, hand-hewn beams, carved armoire-style cabinetry, and stone tile floors set a warm tone, with a hand-finished metal range hood, a pair of antique arched doors, and a wrought-iron staircase along the way. The primary suite features a reclaimed wood accent wall and a copper soaking tub, and out back a pool looks over open pasture.

Cabannis Lane

White board-and-batten siding, a metal roof, and black-framed windows and doors keep the outside clean and simple. Inside, polished concrete floors run throughout, and the kitchen pairs navy cabinetry with a waterfall island, marble-look counters, and a stainless hood over a professional range. Patterned tile shows up on the stair risers and in the scullery, a teal study glows under hand-blown glass pendants, and live edge walnut vanities warm up the baths. There is even a dedicated pet wash station.

Saddle Horn Trail

Painted white brick and sculpted, curved gables give this home its old-world profile, and an arched iron-and-glass front door sets the tone at the entry. The kitchen pairs warm wood cabinetry with a navy island, a navy and wood range hood, and a patterned tile accent behind the cooktop, finished in brass. The primary bath is wrapped in bold marble-look tile with a freestanding tub and a walk-in shower, and the secondary baths bring in patterned tile floors and green glass subway tile.

Old Base Road

Light brick, a crisp black roofline, and tall black-framed windows give this home a classic look with a modern edge. Out back, a covered patio with a stained tongue-and-groove ceiling and exposed timber trusses makes room for outdoor living. Inside, a floating oak hearth runs the length of the living room fireplace, and the kitchen pairs white shaker cabinetry and herringbone tile with a white oak range hood, a professional range, and a waterfall island. The primary suite has a vaulted ceiling with a wood beam and a frameless glass walk-in shower.

Honey Street

A deep covered porch, board-and-batten siding, and a navy front door give this single-story farmhouse an easy, welcoming feel. The open kitchen is built around a large quartz island, soft gray shaker cabinetry, and a patterned tile backsplash, with a walk-in pantry lined in floor-to-ceiling shelving. Wood-look floors, a double vanity with vintage-style lighting, and plenty of natural light carry the same relaxed style through the rest of the home.
